# Week 02: Introduction to AI and Agents

This practical follows the lectures on the introduction to AI and on intelligent agents.
It is a written tutorial rather than a notebook, and the only practical in the course that consists exclusively of questions and answers.

## Reading

Read Chapters 1 and 2 of Russell and Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (AIMA) before the session.
The questions assume you have done the reading, and the session goes through them in turn.

## Tasks

- Bring your answers, since the session is a discussion of them.
